About the course

What it covers

From traditional software to ML to AI agents: evals as experiments designed to estimate random variables. Metrics, ground truth, datasets, judges, drift, and a final session that designs a full experiment. Every session has a hands-on playground.

Who it is for

Engineers, engineering managers and product managers who ship AI products, and executives that want to set their team up for success.

Prerequisites

Curiosity and passion for customer success. No prior coding or stats background required.

At the end you can

Design an eval that says what works and where quality is blocked. Spot the traps: bias, overfitting, noisy judges, drift. Report a result with its uncertainty and the decision it supports.
