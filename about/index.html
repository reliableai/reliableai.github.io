Bio

AI Architect/scientist at ServiceNow and Professor at the University of Trento.

I work on i) conversational knowledge and intent elicitation, ii) agent-centered knowledge management and iii) wellbeing studies in AI agents.

I have published many papers that mostly add noise to the overall body of knowledge, but lately I am getting way better in the signal to noise ratio. My real contributions come from research in the area of wellbeing for older adults, that informs policy decisions in many countries, and recent research in conversational clustering.
